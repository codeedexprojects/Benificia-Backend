BENIFICIA — INFORMATION WE COLLECT FROM USERS
==============================================
This document explains what information we ask users to fill in
on the Benificia app. The goal is to understand their financial
situation so we can give them personalized recommendations.

The process is split into clear steps. Users fill these in one
step at a time — nothing is mandatory except a few marked fields.

All money fields are in Indian Rupees (₹).


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BEFORE THE FINANCIAL QUESTIONS — PERSONAL DETAILS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We first ask the user to fill in basic personal information:

  • Full Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender  (Male / Female / Other / Prefer not to say)
  • Profile Photo  (optional)

  Address:
    • Door no. and Street
    • Landmark  (optional)
    • Locality / Area
    • City, District, State, Pincode
    • Country  (default: India)

  Family & Work:
    • Marital Status  (Single / Married / Divorced / Widowed)
    • Number of people financially dependent on them
    • Ages of their children  (if any)
    • Occupation  (what they do for work)
    • Employer / Company name  (optional)
    • Type of income  (Salaried / Business / Freelance)
    • At what age they plan to retire  (default: 60)
    • Are they the main earner in the family?  (Yes / No)
    • Do their family members fully depend on their income?  (Yes / No)


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1 — INCOME
(How much money comes in every month?)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  • Monthly salary from job
  • Monthly income from business
  • Monthly passive income  (rent received, interest, dividends, etc.)
  • Any other monthly income
  • Annual bonus or incentive  (once a year)
  • How much do they expect their income to grow each year?  (in %)


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2 — EXPENSES
(How much money goes out every month?)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Fixed monthly payments:
  • Rent or home loan EMI
  • Car or vehicle loan EMI
  • Other loan EMIs  (personal, education, etc.)
  • Total insurance premiums being paid monthly

Day-to-day expenses:
  • Groceries and food
  • Electricity, water, internet and other utilities
  • Transport and commuting
  • Medical and healthcare costs

Lifestyle spending:
  • Dining out and entertainment
  • Shopping  (clothes, gadgets, etc.)
  • Children's school fees or tuition
  • Any other regular expenses


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3 — ASSETS & LIABILITIES
(What do they own and what do they owe?)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What they own (current value):
  • Cash in hand and bank savings
  • Fixed deposits  (FDs)
  • Mutual funds and stocks
  • Gold  (physical or sovereign bonds)
  • Property / real estate value
  • EPF or PPF balance  (provident fund)
  • Any other assets

What they owe (amount still to be paid):
  • Home loan remaining balance
  • Vehicle loan remaining balance
  • Personal loan remaining balance
  • Credit card unpaid dues
  • Any other outstanding debts

Existing insurance they already have:
  • Life insurance — total cover amount
  • Health insurance — total cover amount


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4 — FINANCIAL GOALS
(What are they saving or planning for?)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Users pick between 1 and 6 goals from the list below.
For each goal they tell us:

  • What the goal is  (from the list below)
  • How much money they will need  (₹ amount)
  • In how many years they want to achieve it
  • How much they have already saved toward it
  • How important this goal is compared to their other goals

Available goals to choose from:
  1. Emergency Fund        — Save money for unexpected situations
  2. Child's Education     — Plan for children's college or higher studies
  3. Buy a House           — Save to purchase a home or property
  4. Retirement            — Build savings to live comfortably after retiring
  5. Wealth Creation       — Grow money through investments
  6. Repay Debts           — Pay off existing loans faster


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 5 — RISK APPETITE
(How comfortable are they with financial risk?)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

We ask 5 simple questions to understand how the user feels about
risk in investments. Each question has 4 options to choose from.

Based on the answers, the user is placed into one of three groups:

  CONSERVATIVE
  — Prefers safety over growth.
  — Does not want to risk losing money.
  — Comfortable with lower but stable returns.

  MODERATE
  — Comfortable with some ups and downs.
  — Wants a balance between safety and growth.

  AGGRESSIVE
  — Comfortable with high risk for potentially higher returns.
  — Okay with short-term losses for long-term gains.

Note: The questions will be written in simple, everyday language
on the app. The client can review and customise the exact wording.

Sample question areas:
  1. How long have you been investing?
  2. What would you do if your investment dropped by 20% suddenly?
  3. Which type of investment would you prefer?
  4. When do you think you will need this money?
  5. How stable and secure is your current income?


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT (Phase 1)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The following are NOT required in the first version of the app:

  • Aadhaar card or any identity verification
  • PAN number
  • Bank account or UPI details
  • Tax returns or income proof documents
  • Credit score


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW THIS INFORMATION IS USED
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All the information collected is used only to generate personalized
financial recommendations for the user — suggesting the right
insurance plans and investment options based on their specific
income, expenses, goals, and risk comfort level.

No information is shared with third parties without user consent.


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FOR THE CLIENT — THINGS YOU CAN CHANGE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  • Any field in any step can be added, removed, or renamed easily.
  • The 5 risk questions and their answer options can be fully
    customised — just share the question text and we will update it.
